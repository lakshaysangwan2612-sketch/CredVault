Trust, but verify — mathematically

Every credential is hashed and signed at issuance. Nobody — not even the student — can edit one without the proof breaking.

  • Tamper-evident: any edit to a record breaks its SHA-256 hash and fails verification.
  • Offline-verifiable: signatures check against the published institution public key.
  • Revocation control: admins can revoke a credential; portfolios and verifiers see it instantly.
  • Privacy by default: students choose which credentials appear on their public portfolio.
